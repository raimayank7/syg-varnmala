Varnmala

About Varnmala

Varnmala by ShareYourGroup is a free site for learning the letters of two Indian scripts: the Hindi varnmala (Devanagari, 52 letters as most school charts count them) and the Punjabi alphabet (Gurmukhi, 41 letters). Every Hindi letter has its own page with a picture word, more words, a small rhyme and notes on its shape. The Punjabi letters are taught row by row with their traditional names. You can trace any letter on the screen or print tracing sheets.

Where the content comes from

The alphabets themselves are the standard ones taught in Indian schools. The order of the letters, the traditional names of the Gurmukhi letters and the classification of sounds are common knowledge found in every school primer. The example words, meanings, rhymes, shape notes and guides were written for this site; nothing is copied from a textbook, chart or website.

What this site does not do

It does not show animated stroke-by-stroke drawings of each letter. Handwriting models differ a little between schools and states, so the site gives the general order of strokes and a description of each shape, and asks you to follow the child's own school book where it differs. The sound you hear comes from the voice of your own device; many devices have a Hindi voice, and few have a Punjabi one.

Language note

The Hindi and Punjabi words were written with care in everyday language, but they have not yet been read by a second native speaker. If something is wrong, please tell us. Last reviewed in November 2026.
